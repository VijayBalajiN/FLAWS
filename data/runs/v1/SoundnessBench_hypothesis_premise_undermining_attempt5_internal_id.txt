This is a serious conceptual error because it incorrectly equates "methodological soundness" with "viability for publication." The research plan is designed to test an LLM's ability to assess methodological soundness, using reviewer soundness sub-scores as the ground truth. However, a paper's ultimate viability for publication depends on many other factors not measured in this experiment, such as novelty, significance of results, and clarity. The experiment explicitly removes results, which are a primary determinant of publication success, creating a fundamental mismatch between what the hypothesis claims to test and what the experiment actually measures.
