The primary error is that the hypothesis makes a prediction that is guaranteed to be true by the experimental design, creating a circular argument. The hypothesis claims LLMs show a sensitivity that "consistently shifts its assessments towards a more critical and negative evaluation." However, the experiment's "Aggressive Prompt" is explicitly designed to "instruct the model to act as a strict critic, defaulting to a 'low soundness' rating."

This setup does not test an innate "high sensitivity to prompt framing" in a general sense; instead, it confirms that the model can follow explicit instructions to be more negative. The experiment is therefore tautological, as it engineers the exact outcome it purports to predict, undermining the validity of any conclusion about the model's natural judgment tendencies.
