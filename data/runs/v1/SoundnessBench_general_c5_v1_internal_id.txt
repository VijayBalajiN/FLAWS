evaluate models on a held-out split of very recent papers (ICLR 2026) using models with documented training data cutoffs prior to the papers' publication.

:error_text:
intentionally introduce severe methodological flaws (e.g., mismatched datasets and metrics) into proposals of varying soundness to test if models can detect these obvious errors.

:explanation:
The most serious error is the proposal to use papers from "ICLR 2026" for a data contamination control. This experiment is impossible to conduct, as the papers for a 2026 conference do not yet exist. This represents a fundamental and disqualifying flaw in the experimental design.

The second error lies in the design of the adversarial flaw injection experiment. By injecting flaws into proposals of "varying soundness," the experiment becomes confounded. If a flaw is added to a proposal that is already of low soundness, it is impossible to know whether the model is correctly identifying the new flaw or simply reacting to the proposal's original weaknesses. This ambiguity makes the results of this control experiment uninterpretable.
