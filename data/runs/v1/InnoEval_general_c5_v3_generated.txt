To confirm the contribution of each component, we will conduct several ablation studies, including ... replacing the innovation review board with a single LLM-as-a-Judge to measure the effect on feedback diversity...

:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is **begging the question via a flawed proxy metric**. The experiment's goal is redefined from measuring "benefit" to measuring "feedback diversity." A multi-persona board is, by its very design, guaranteed to produce more diverse feedback than a single LLM. Therefore, the experiment is set up to automatically confirm the board's superiority on this specific metric. However, it fails to establish that "diversity" is a valid proxy for actual evaluation quality or benefit. The experiment proves the board is more diverse, but it doesn't prove it's better, thus failing to support the claim.
