:error_text:
exhibiting a bias towards optimism in its textual ratings

:explanation:
This phrase creates a fundamental mismatch between the hypothesis and the experimental design. The experiment is designed to evaluate the LLM's performance on a binary classification task (labeling a proposal as "high" or "low" soundness), with metrics like the false-positive rate and F1 score derived from these labels. However, the hypothesis claims the bias will be found "in its textual ratings," which implies an analysis of the model's free-text justification. The proposed experiment does not measure the nature of the text itself, but rather the final classification choice, thus failing to directly test this part of the hypothesis.
