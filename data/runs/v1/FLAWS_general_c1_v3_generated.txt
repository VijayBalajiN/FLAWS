:original_text:
The criticality of an error in a scientific paper is a function of its connection to the paper's core claims.

:modified_text:
The retraction likelihood of a scientific paper is a function of an error's connection to the paper's core claims.

:explanation:
This introduces a category error by substituting the abstract concept of "criticality" (an error's scientific importance) with a concrete procedural outcome ("retraction likelihood"). Retraction is influenced by many factors orthogonal to an error's relationship with the core scientific claims, such as plagiarism, data fabrication, or ethical violations in the methods section. Therefore, the modified claim is false because the primary determinant has been incorrectly identified for this new, distinct category.
