The most serious error is that the experiment design is based on non-existent, speculative resources, making it impossible to execute. The plan proposes using a corpus of papers from a future conference (ICML 2025) and models that have not been released (Gemini 2.5 Pro, GPT 5). This is a fundamental flaw that invalidates the entire experimental plan as it cannot be implemented.

A secondary, but also critical, methodological flaw exists in the human validation process. By providing annotators with the "full generation context," the study removes the blindness required for objective evaluation. The annotators would know precisely how and where the error was inserted, making their assessment of an LLM's identification trivial rather than a valid judgment. This undermines the validation of the automated evaluation metric, which is a cornerstone of the proposed contribution.
