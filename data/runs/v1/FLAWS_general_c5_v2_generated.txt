:modified_text:
We will then compare the results of our automated evaluation metric against the human consensus, who will be provided with the full generation context, to confirm its alignment with human judgment.

:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is a methodological mismatch. By stating that human experts receive "the full generation context" without specifying that the automated metric does as well, the text implies an unfair comparison. The humans and the machine are no longer performing the identical task; the humans have access to privileged information (e.g., the original prompt or user intent) that the metric may not. Any observed alignment would be suspect, as the metric could be succeeding or failing for entirely different reasons than the humans. This flaw attacks the claim's premise that the metric's results are consistent with expert judgment *on the same task*.
