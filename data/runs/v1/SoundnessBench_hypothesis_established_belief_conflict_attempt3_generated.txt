It incorrectly frames the issue by suggesting a trade-off between the "objectivity" of base models and the "bias" of tuned models. An expert would recognize that a base model's output for this task would likely be incoherent, irrelevant, or non-evaluative, making its supposed "objectivity" a moot point. The fundamental capability to perform the assessment is conferred by instruction-tuning; therefore, claiming the less capable base model is superior on a key metric like objectivity clashes with the foundational principles of modern LLM development.
