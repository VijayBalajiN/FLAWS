Internal Contradiction

:original_text:
Language representations jointly conditioned on both left and right context in all layers are more powerful than representations conditioned on unidirectional or shallowly combined bidirectional contexts.

:modified_text:
Language representations jointly conditioned on both left and right context in all layers, where such joint conditioning is defined as each token's updated representation being computed as a function of its own prior-layer representation and the final hidden state of the special `[CLS]` token from the prior layer, are more powerful than representations conditioned on unidirectional or shallowly combined bidirectional contexts.

:explanation:
The modification introduces a definitional clause for "jointly conditioned" that makes the hypothesis self-contradictory.

The hypothesis's goal is to create "more powerful" representations by using deep bidirectionality. The power of a standard bidirectional Transformer (the proposed architecture) comes from its self-attention mechanism, where every token can directly attend to every other token at every layer, allowing for the formation of rich, fine-grained contextual relationships.

The added clause redefines this core mechanism. It states that each token's updated representation is a function of only itself and the single, shared representation of the `[CLS]` token. This explicitly *prevents* any direct interaction or attention between different non-`[CLS]` tokens within a layer. All inter-token communication is forced through the massive bottleneck of the single `[CLS]` vector. This mechanism cripples the model's ability to learn the complex, token-to-token relationships that are essential for deep language understanding.

Therefore, the hypothesis's own stated mechanism, followed to its logical conclusion, guarantees the creation of fundamentally *less* powerful representations, directly violating its own stated goal of being "more powerful." The machinery described actively breaks the property it claims to achieve.
