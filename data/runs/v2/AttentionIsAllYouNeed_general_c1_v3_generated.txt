We will compute the dot products of Q with all K, divide each by √d_k, apply a softmax function to obtain weights, and then compute a weighted sum of V.

:modified_text:
We will compute the dot products of Q with all K, apply a softmax function to obtain weights, and then compute a weighted sum of V. This final weighted sum will be scaled by dividing it by √d_k.

:explanation:
This flaw misapplies the crucial scaling factor in the attention mechanism. The scaling by `1/√d_k` is meant to be applied to the logits (the dot products of Q and K) *before* the softmax function. This prevents the dot products' magnitude from pushing the softmax into saturated regions where gradients are near zero, which would stall learning. By moving the scaling step to *after* the weighted sum is computed, this critical stabilization is removed, making the model training unstable and likely to fail. This undermines the claim that the proposed architecture is "sufficient."
