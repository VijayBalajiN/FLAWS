This modification introduces a conceptual misinterpretation. It incorrectly claims that RMSF, a measure of the average magnitude of atomic displacement, can "directly characterize the diffusional nature" of motion. RMSF is a static, time-averaged value that cannot distinguish between different types of dynamics, such as harmonic vibrations versus diffusional jumps. Characterizing diffusional motion requires analyzing the time-dependence of the motion (e.g., via mean-squared displacement vs. time), so by misattributing this capability to RMSF, the plan renders the measure insufficient for testing the central hypothesis.
