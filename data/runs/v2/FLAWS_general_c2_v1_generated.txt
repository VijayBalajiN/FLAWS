:modified_text:
To create the FLAWS benchmark, we will use two state-of-the-art LLMs (e.g., Gemini 2.5 Pro and GPT 5), both derived from similar foundational architectures, for the error insertion process. This will mitigate model-specific biases in error generation.

:explanation:
This modification undermines the claim by suggesting the two LLMs are not as "distinct" as assumed. Stating they are "derived from similar foundational architectures" introduces the high probability of correlated biases and shared failure modes. The mitigation of bias would therefore be insufficient because the models are likely to share conceptual blind spots, a subtle but critical flaw that invalidates the assumption of their independence. The underlying mechanism of error is **correlated bias**.
