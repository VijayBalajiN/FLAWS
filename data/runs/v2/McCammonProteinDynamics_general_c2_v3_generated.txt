1.  **Initialization:** We will use the atomic coordinates from the published X-ray structure as the initial positions for all atoms. To maintain the native fold, strong harmonic positional restraints will be applied to all backbone alpha-carbon atoms throughout the simulation. Initial velocities will be set to zero.

:explanation:
This modification introduces an artificial constraint on the system's dynamics. Applying strong positional restraints to all backbone alpha-carbons effectively freezes the protein's overall structure, preventing the very "fluid-like" collective motions and conformational changes the study hypothesizes. The simulation would only capture trivial, localized vibrations around a fixed backbone. The claim that the duration is sufficient to capture "essential dynamical characteristics" is therefore false, because the most essential dynamics have been artificially removed from the system by the experimental setup itself.
