:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is an **artificial constraint on the data's complexity**. By filtering the dataset to only include short sentences (<=25 words), the experiment removes the primary challenge that makes parsing a good generalization test: modeling complex, long-range dependencies and deep hierarchical structures. The Transformer's core architectural hypothesis is its superior ability to handle such dependencies compared to recurrent models. Testing it only on short sentences where these challenges are minimal makes the task an insufficient test of this capability. The results would not provide strong evidence for the model's generalization, as the problem has been artificially simplified to avoid the very difficulties it was meant to address.
