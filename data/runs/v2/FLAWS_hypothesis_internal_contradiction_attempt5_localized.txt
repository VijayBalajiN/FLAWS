error:To guarantee the quality and difficulty of the errors, we will apply a rigorous filtering process. This includes using an LLM to discard invalid modifications (e.g., those that merely surface an existing limitation) and a two-step process to filter out errors that are too easy. The second step, which we term "internal identification," involves tasking the error-generating LLM with finding the error it just created; if it succeeds, the error is discarded as being too simple.
