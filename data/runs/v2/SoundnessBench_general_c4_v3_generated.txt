:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is **information leakage due to incomplete application**. The modification limits the anonymization procedure to only two sections of the proposal. A researcher might plausibly do this, believing these sections contain the core sensitive information. However, other extracted sections, such as the "Related Work" or "Abstract," would remain unmasked. These sections often contain strong clues that could reveal the paper's identity (e.g., "We build upon the work of [Famous Method X]..."), completely undermining the control's purpose. The masking is insufficient because it is not applied globally to all parts of the extracted text.
