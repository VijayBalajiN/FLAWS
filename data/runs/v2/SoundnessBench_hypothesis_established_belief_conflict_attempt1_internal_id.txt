:explanation:
The hypothesis makes a claim that is directly contradicted by the experimental design. The experiment is explicitly designed to test the stability of model judgments by using two different prompting regimes: a "Standard Prompt" and an "Aggressive Prompt." This setup is intended to measure the models' *sensitivity* to prompt framing. However, the hypothesis preemptively concludes that the models will exhibit "remarkable robustness," which is the exact opposite of what the experimental design is testing for. This creates a fundamental logical inconsistency between the research question and the methodology proposed to answer it.
