:original_text:
Two human annotators will assess (1) the validity of the inserted scientific errors and (2) the correctness of the LLMs' identifications.

:modified_text:
Two human annotators will assess the validity of the inserted scientific errors.

:explanation:
The flaw introduced is a `Missing intermediate step` within the validation protocol. The modified text removes the crucial task for human annotators to assess the correctness of the LLMs' identifications. This breaks the logical chain required to validate the automated evaluation metric. The subsequent sentence states the plan is to "compare the results of our automated evaluation metric against the human consensus to confirm its alignment with human judgment." However, because the annotators are no longer tasked with evaluating the LLM outputs, no "human consensus" on model correctness is ever generated. This makes the promised comparison impossible and leaves the entire automated evaluation framework unvalidated against a human gold standard.
