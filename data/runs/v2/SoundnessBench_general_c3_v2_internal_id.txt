The second error is a methodological flaw in the design of the "Aggressive Prompt." By explicitly instructing the model to default to a "low soundness" rating, the experiment introduces a major confound. It becomes a test of the model's ability to follow a complex instruction with a default condition, rather than a pure measure of its inherent critical reasoning or skepticism. This makes it difficult to validly interpret the results from this prompt.
