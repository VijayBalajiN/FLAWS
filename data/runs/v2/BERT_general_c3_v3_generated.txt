This introduces a confounding variable in the model architecture itself, separate from scale. By specifying a more advanced activation function (GELU) for `BERT_LARGE` and leaving it unmentioned for `BERT_BASE`, it implies the base model uses a more standard, less effective activation like ReLU. This means the two models differ in ways other than just size. Any performance difference could be attributed to the superior activation function, not the model's scale, undermining the claim.
