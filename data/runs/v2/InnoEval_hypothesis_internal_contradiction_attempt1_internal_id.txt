:error_text:
each perspective is independently grounded in a disjoint subset of evidence

:error_text:
to ensure maximal cognitive independence

:explanation:
The hypothesis makes a specific, falsifiable claim that cognitive independence is achieved by grounding each perspective in a "disjoint subset of evidence." However, the Method section contradicts this by proposing to "partially mask the retrieved knowledge." Partial masking allows for overlapping evidence sets, which are by definition not disjoint. This is a critical flaw, as the experiment is not designed to test the actual hypothesis. Furthermore, the goal of "maximal cognitive independence" is an overstatement, as the proposed partial masking method is a weaker form of separation than using the disjoint sets claimed in the hypothesis.
