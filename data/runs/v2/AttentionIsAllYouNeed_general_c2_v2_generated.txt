:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is an **inappropriate evaluation metric**. BLEU score, which measures n-gram overlap, is fundamentally unsuited for evaluating the syntactic correctness of a hierarchical structure like a parse tree. It cannot distinguish between a well-formed tree and one with major structural errors (e.g., unbalanced parentheses, incorrect constituent spans) as long as the local token sequences are similar. Using BLEU makes the evaluation a test of surface-level string matching, not structural understanding. This invalidates the claim that the task is a sufficient test of generalization to structured prediction, as the method of measuring success is blind to the core structural aspect of the problem.
