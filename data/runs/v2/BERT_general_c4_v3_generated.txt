To handle sub-word tokenization, we will average the embeddings for all WordPieces within a single original word and feed this single averaged vector per word into a BiLSTM classifier, comparing its performance to the standard fine-tuning approach.

:explanation:
This error introduces a flaw in the data processing and feature engineering step. BERT uses WordPiece tokenization, which breaks words into sub-word units. To perform word-level NER, these sub-word representations must be aggregated. While averaging is a possible strategy, it is often suboptimal because it can wash out important discriminative information present in the individual sub-word embeddings (e.g., the first WordPiece often carries the most semantic weight). By mandating this naive and lossy aggregation method, the experiment systematically degrades the quality of the input for the feature-based approach, handicapping its performance and creating a biased comparison that would unfairly favor the fine-tuning method.
