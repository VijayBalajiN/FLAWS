:error_type:
Internal Contradiction

:original_text:
The interior of a folded globular protein is a fluid-like environment where local atomic motions exhibit a diffusional character.

:modified_text:
The interior of a folded globular protein is a fluid-like environment where local atomic motions exhibit a diffusional character, governed by a potential energy surface where the time-averaged force on each atom is linearly proportional to its displacement from the mean.

:explanation:
The modification introduces a mechanism that directly contradicts the phenomenon the hypothesis claims to describe. The original hypothesis posits a "fluid-like environment" with "diffusional character," which implies atoms can move freely from their starting positions in a random walk. The added clause, however, states that the force on an atom is "linearly proportional to its displacement from the mean." This is the definition of Hooke's Law (F = -kx), which governs a simple harmonic oscillator. Motion in such a potential is purely oscillatory and confined; an atom will only vibrate around its mean position. This is the characteristic behavior of atoms in a solid, not a fluid. Therefore, the stated mechanism (harmonic restoring forces) makes the stated outcome (diffusional motion) impossible, creating a self-contained contradiction.
