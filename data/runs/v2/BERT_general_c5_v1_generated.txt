For sentence-level classification tasks (MNLI, QQP, QNLI, SST-2, etc.), we will format single-sentence inputs like SST-2 as a pair with an empty second sentence and feed the final hidden state of the `[CLS]` token into a new classification layer.

:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is a **mismatch between the pre-training objective and the downstream task application**. The modification implies that the `[CLS]` token representation is only meaningful in the context of a sentence-pair structure, which it was trained for during the Next Sentence Prediction (NSP) task. By forcing single-sentence tasks into this format, the proposal suggests the `[CLS]` token is not a truly sufficient aggregate representation of a single sequence on its own, but rather a specialized representation for sentence-pair relationships. This undermines its general sufficiency for all claimed sentence-level tasks.
