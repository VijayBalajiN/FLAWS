:modified_text:
analyzing the inter-correlations between the evaluation dimensions to see if they align with human cognitive patterns, as derived from the inter-correlations of human reviewer scores within our OpenReview dataset.

:explanation:
This introduces a circular validation flaw. The claim is that the model's emergent reasoning mirrors general human cognitive patterns. However, this modified plan proposes to validate this by checking if the model's dimension correlations match the correlations found in the human scores of the *specific dataset* being used. A model could simply overfit to the statistical artifacts of this particular dataset, perfectly reproducing its score correlations without learning any generalizable cognitive patterns. This makes the validation a test of dataset mimicry rather than a genuine test of alignment with human reasoning.
