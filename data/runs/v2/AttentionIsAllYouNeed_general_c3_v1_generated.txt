:original_text:
We will vary the learning rate over the course of training according to the formula: `lr = d_model^-0.5 * min(step_num^-0.5, step_num * warmup_steps^-1.5)`.

:modified_text:
We will vary the learning rate over the course of training according to the formula: `lr = d_model^-0.5 * max(step_num^-0.5, step_num * warmup_steps^-1.5)`.

:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is a subtle but critical typographical flaw in the mathematical formula. The `min` function, which correctly implements a warm-up phase followed by a decay phase, has been replaced with `max`. This inverts the schedule's logic. Instead of a linear increase followed by an inverse square root decay, the learning rate will now follow the decay curve during the warm-up phase and then switch to the linear *increase* term, causing it to grow without bound. This will inevitably lead to training divergence and catastrophic model failure, directly falsifying the claim that this is an effective optimization strategy.
