This modification introduces a subtle confound. By using a much smaller and less capable model (T5-small) to rewrite a paragraph within a proposal written by human experts, the injected text will almost certainly have a different stylistic and complexity profile. The test would therefore not be a valid proxy for detecting subtle, naturally occurring flaws, as it's actually measuring the LLM's ability to detect stylistic artifacts introduced by the injection process.
