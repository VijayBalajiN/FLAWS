The mechanism of error is flawed thermostatting. The use of periodic velocity rescaling (a simple Berendsen-type thermostat) is known to not generate a correct canonical (NVT) ensemble. This directly undermines the claim that the system will achieve a "thermally representative dynamic state," as the statistical properties of that state will be incorrect.

The subsequent production run would be dominated by these initial-state artifacts.

By harmonically restraining all heavy atoms *to* their initial positions, the protocol actively prevents this relaxation from occurring. While the system's kinetic energy might be raised, the potential energy stresses are locked in by the artificial restraints. This directly falsifies the claim that the equilibration is sufficient "to relax the stresses inherent in the static X-ray crystal structure."
