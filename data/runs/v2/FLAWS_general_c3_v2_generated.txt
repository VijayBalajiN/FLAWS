:modified_text:
We will source our papers from the accepted list of the 2025 International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), ensuring they were first posted on arXiv after the publicly stated pre-training knowledge cutoff dates of the models we plan to test.

:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is a misunderstanding of how model knowledge cutoffs work. Specifying "publicly stated pre-training" knowledge cutoffs sounds rigorous but is misleading. State-of-the-art models often undergo continuous fine-tuning or reinforcement learning (RLHF) on data much newer than their base pre-training corpus. The model being evaluated via an API may have been updated with data that post-dates the pre-training cutoff, including the very papers selected for the benchmark. This falsifies the claim by failing to account for the model's full, evolving training data history.
