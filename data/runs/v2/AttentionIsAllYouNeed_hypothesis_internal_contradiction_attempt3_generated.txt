:explanation:
The modification introduces an internal contradiction by redefining the proposed mechanism in a way that directly conflicts with its stated goal within the same sentence.

The stated goal is to "avoid[...] the sequential computation inherent in recurrent models," a key motivation for the entire research plan.

However, the modified text describes the mechanism for achieving this as "sequentially iterating through all possible pairs of input positions to compute attention scores." This described mechanism is, by its own definition, a form of sequential computation. Iterating through N-squared pairs one by one reintroduces a severe sequential bottleneck, the very problem the architecture claims to solve.

Therefore, the hypothesis's own stated mechanism (sequential iteration) makes it impossible to achieve its own stated goal (avoiding sequential computation). The contradiction is self-contained within the logic of the modified hypothesis statement.
