Since `d_ff` is four times larger than `d_model`, the scaling factor `d_ff^-0.5` will be half the size of the intended `d_model^-0.5`. This results in a learning rate that is consistently 50% smaller than intended across the entire training process. Such a significant reduction could lead to extremely slow convergence or cause the model to get stuck in a poor local minimum, undermining the schedule's effectiveness.
