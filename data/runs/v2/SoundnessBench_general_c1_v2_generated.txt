:modified_text:
We will then assign binary labels—"high soundness" (mean score ≥ 6) or "low soundness" (mean score ≤ 4)—based on the average reviewer overall recommendation score, excluding ambiguous cases.

:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is flawed operationalization, where the proxy for the ground truth is invalid. The modification replaces the specific "soundness sub-score" with the "overall recommendation score" for labeling data. An overall score is confounded by many factors other than methodological soundness, such as perceived novelty, impact, or clarity. A proposal could be methodologically sound but receive a low overall score for being incremental. If an LLM correctly identifies such a proposal as sound, the experiment would wrongly count it as a false positive, artifactually inflating the "optimism bias" metric. The claim is undermined because the experiment is no longer measuring what it claims to be measuring.
