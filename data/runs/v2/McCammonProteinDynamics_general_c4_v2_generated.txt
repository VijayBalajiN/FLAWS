:modified_text:
Our simulation will be based on an empirical potential energy function, which represents the total energy as a sum of terms associated with bond lengths, bond angles, dihedral angles, hydrogen bonds, and non-bonded (van der Waals and electrostatic) interactions, using a rigid, non-polarizable three-point model for the internal water molecules.

:explanation:
This introduces a subtle parameterization error in the force field. While common for bulk solvent, using a simple, non-polarizable water model for a few key internal molecules is problematic. These waters are in a unique, low-dielectric environment where polarization effects and subtle hydrogen-bond dynamics are critical for mediating protein motions. A rigid, non-polarizable model cannot capture these effects, leading to an inaccurate representation of the forces and interactions within the protein core. This undermines the claim of an "accurate" simulation by using a physical model inadequate for the specific chemical environment being studied.
