:modified_text:
analyzing the inter-correlations between the evaluation dimensions—using the final meta-review's assessment of significance as a proxy for the validity dimension—to see if they align with human cognitive patterns.

:explanation:
This introduces a severe measurement flaw (a construct validity error) directly into the analysis pipeline. By explicitly using the "significance" score as a stand-in for the "validity" score during this specific analysis, the correlation between these two dimensions is artificially forced to be perfect (r=1.0). The analysis would then be "validating" a pre-determined artifact of its own flawed methodology, not an emergent property of the model. This makes the entire analysis meaningless for testing the claim, as it's no longer measuring the relationship between two independent assessments.
