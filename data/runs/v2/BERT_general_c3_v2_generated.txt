:original_text:
We will compare the performance of `BERT_BASE` and `BERT_LARGE` across all tasks to measure the impact of model scale on downstream performance, especially on smaller datasets.

:modified_text:
We will compare the performance of `BERT_BASE` using standard hyperparameters against a fully-tuned `BERT_LARGE` across all tasks to measure the impact of model scale on downstream performance, especially on smaller datasets.

:explanation:
This introduces a systematic bias in the experimental procedure. By explicitly stating that only `BERT_LARGE` will be "fully-tuned" while `BERT_BASE` uses "standard" (implying sub-optimal) hyperparameters, the comparison is no longer fair. The mechanism of error is an unfair optimization process; any performance gap could be due to the difference in tuning effort rather than the difference in model scale.
