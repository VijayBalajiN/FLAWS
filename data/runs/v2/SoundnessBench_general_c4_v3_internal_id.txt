As this conference has not yet occurred, these papers do not exist, making this critical part of the research plan impossible to execute.

A second, less severe error lies in the design of the "Aggressive Prompt." By explicitly instructing the model to "default" to a negative rating, the experiment creates a strong demand characteristic. This confounds the measurement of the model's inherent bias or critical reasoning ability with its ability to simply follow a directive, potentially undermining the validity of conclusions drawn from this experimental condition.
