:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is **conceptual inference**. The modification narrows the scope of masking to only the most explicit identifiers (the new method's acronym and author names). This is a plausible but flawed approach because it leaves the rich conceptual context intact. An LLM could easily de-anonymize the paper by recognizing the unique combination of cited prior work, the specific problem formulation, and the technical description of the (unnamed) methodology, thereby invalidating the control. The model would still be using "recognition" of the paper's core ideas, not "reasoning" about its methodology from first principles.
