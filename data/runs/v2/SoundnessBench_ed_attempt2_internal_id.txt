:explanation:
The most serious error is a temporal impossibility that makes a key control experiment unexecutable: the plan proposes using papers from ICLR 2026, a conference that has not yet occurred and for which no papers exist.

The second error undermines the validity of the prompt sensitivity test. By instructing the model to "default" to a low rating, the "Aggressive Prompt" tests the model's ability to follow an explicit rule rather than its capacity for more stringent scientific reasoning. This conflates instruction-following with a genuine shift in critical judgment.

The third error weakens the "Adversarial Flaw Injection" control. Using a "templated paragraph" to introduce flaws means the model can achieve high performance by simply pattern-matching the template's text, rather than by understanding and identifying the underlying methodological error. This tests surface-level recognition instead of the intended reasoning capability.
