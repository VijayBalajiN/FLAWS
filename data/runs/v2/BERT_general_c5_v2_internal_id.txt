This describes a flawed fine-tuning procedure for the GLUE classification tasks. The standard and intended method for BERT is to use only the final hidden state of the `[CLS]` token as the aggregate sequence representation for classification. The model's pre-training (specifically the Next Sentence Prediction task) is designed to make this single vector meaningful. Concatenating it with a max-pooled representation is an unnecessary and complex modification that contradicts the "minimal task-specific output layer" principle, potentially introducing noise and making the results incomparable to the standard BERT fine-tuning approach.
