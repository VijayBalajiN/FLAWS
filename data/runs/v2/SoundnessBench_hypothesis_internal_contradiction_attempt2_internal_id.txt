:error_text:

:explanation:
The core error is a direct contradiction between the mechanism proposed in the hypothesis and the experiment designed to test it. The hypothesis claims that prompt framing "consistently amplifies the model's default evaluative stance [optimism] regardless of the frame's valence." This implies that even a negatively-valenced prompt should make the model *more* optimistic.

However, the Experiment Design introduces an "Aggressive Prompt" which is explicitly designed to do the opposite: to make the model a "strict critic" and induce a bias towards "low soundness" ratings. The experiment is set up to test if a negative frame can *reduce* or *reverse* the optimism bias, not amplify or exacerbate it. This makes the central causal claim of the hypothesis inconsistent with the methodology.
