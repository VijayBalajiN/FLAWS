:explanation:
The most serious error is a practical impossibility within the experimental design. The contamination control experiment proposes using papers from "ICLR 2026," a conference that has not yet occurred, making this crucial robustness check non-executable as written.

The second error is a conceptual flaw in the design of the "Aggressive Prompt." By explicitly instructing the model to "default" to a low rating, the experiment conflates the model's ability to follow instructions with its capacity for scientific judgment. This makes it difficult to interpret whether a resulting bias is evidence of unreliable assessment or simply accurate instruction-following, undermining a key part of the experimental setup designed to test the central hypothesis.
