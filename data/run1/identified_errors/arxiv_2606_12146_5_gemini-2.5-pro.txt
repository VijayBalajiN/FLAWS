The paper's central claims about superior and consistent extrapolation performance are directly contradicted by its own experimental results.

1.  **Catastrophic Failure Compared to Baselines:** In image and video resolution extrapolation (Tables 1 and 2), nD-RoPE's performance collapses dramatically at high resolutions and is significantly worse than the standard learnable absolute positional embedding (APE) baseline. For instance, on ImageNet at 1024x1024 resolution, nD-RoPE achieves 35.51% accuracy, whereas the learnable PE baseline from DeiT-S reaches 50.99%. A similar catastrophic drop is seen on Kinetics-400 (29.21% for nD-RoPE vs. 54.23% for learnable PE). This invalidates the claim of improved extrapolation "over existing positional embedding methods."

2.  **Inconsistent Performance:** The claim of "consistently slower performance degradation" is falsified in Table 4 (SemanticKITTI), where at the coarsest grid resolution (0.15), nD-RoPE (53.53% mIoU) is outperformed by "3D RoPE-Axial + APE" (54.19% mIoU).

The textual claims of robust, stable, and superior extrapolation are not supported by the data. The paper selectively emphasizes comparisons against weaker RoPE variants while ignoring a standard baseline that performs much better, and makes claims of consistency that are demonstrably false. This is a major flaw that undermines one of the paper's key advertised contributions.
