The paper's central claim is that multi-agent LLM debates exhibit a phase transition (or a finite-size crossover) where a strong collective bias emerges from the amplification of weak individual biases at low sampling temperatures (noise). The primary evidence for this claim, the empirical phase diagrams in Figure 2 (c, d, e), directly contradicts this.

In these plots, for weak individual biases (small values of `b` on the y-axis), the collective norm `|m(R)|` remains low (blue/green colors) across all temperatures, including very low ones (right side of the x-axis). This shows a lack of amplification. A strong collective norm only appears when the initial individual bias `b` is already large. This is a trivial outcome, not the "emergence" of a norm from a weak signal, which is the hallmark of a phase transition.
