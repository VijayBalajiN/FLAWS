:explanation:
The paper presents a major internal contradiction regarding one of its central, nuanced findings: the model-dependent self-correction of factual contamination.

One of the paper's key claims, presented in Section 4.6 and its accompanying Table 5, is that persistence is type-dependent and that Llama-3.1-8B consistently self-corrects factual contamination, resulting in a 0% contamination rate for that specific type. This is highlighted as a critical finding, distinguishing it from other models.

However, the Appendix C.1 ("Scaling Analysis") presents data and claims that are in direct opposition. Table 10 reports a 100% overall contamination rate for Llama-3.1-8B. The text then explicitly and incorrectly attempts to justify this number by stating that *all* contamination types, including factual, yielded 100% contamination for this model. This claim is irreconcilable with the results from Section 4.6. If 4 out of the 10 tested scenarios (the factual ones) result in 0% contamination as claimed in the main text, the overall rate for Llama-3.1-8B cannot mathematically be 100%.

This contradiction between two different experiments on the same model is a fatal flaw. It invalidates a key finding and undermines the credibility of the entire empirical evaluation, as the paper is simultaneously claiming a model has both 0% and 100% contamination under the same type of attack.
