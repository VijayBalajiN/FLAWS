A prediction with perfect precision (`p=1`) means it has zero false positives or mismatches. For any non-empty prediction with `p=1`, the number of saved user actions (`ℓ`) must be at least one, because the prediction correctly performs tasks the user would have otherwise done manually, and no corrective actions are needed. Therefore, any prediction accepted by the `p100` heuristic (which requires `p=1`) must also be accepted by the `greedy` heuristic (which requires `ℓ ≥ 1`).
