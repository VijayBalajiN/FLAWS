The paper defines conditional binding information as $\mathbf{B}_{\mathcal{O, F}}^{*}(Z) := I(O;Z \mid F)$. By definition, conditional mutual information must be non-negative. However, Theorem 2.11 and its entropy-based expansion in Theorem 2.12 incorrectly state that this quantity is equal to $I(F;Z) - I(O;Z)$.
