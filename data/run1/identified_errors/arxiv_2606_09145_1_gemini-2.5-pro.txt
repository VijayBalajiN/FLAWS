If "frozen" implies detaching gradients, it means the junior model's parameters are not updated based on the sensitive prompts. This would severely undermine the model's ability to learn the mapping from prompts to code, calling into question both the utility of the generated code and the meaningfulness of the privacy guarantee for prompts.
