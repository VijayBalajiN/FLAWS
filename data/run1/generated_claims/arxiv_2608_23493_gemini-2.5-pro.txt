Based on the provided LaTeX source, here is a list of the major, unique, and falsifiable claims made in the paper:

1.  The SRPO framework, which uses a model's self-generated "reflection patches" to create a teacher policy, transforms sparse terminal rewards into dense, token-level supervision for on-policy training without needing an external teacher model.

2.  Applied to a Qwen3-8B model, SRPO achieves a higher score on the AIME'24 mathematical reasoning benchmark (73.3%) than standard reinforcement learning methods like GRPO (68.0%) and on-policy distillation from a larger Qwen3-32B teacher (70.0%).

3.  SRPO improves success rates on long-horizon agentic tasks, with a Qwen3-8B model achieving 64.7% on WebShop, 76.8% on ALFWorld, and 31.2% on SWE-Bench-Lite, outperforming baseline methods including GRPO and SFT.

4.  SRPO allows a model to improve upon the guidance of a larger teacher; an SRPO-trained Qwen3-8B model (73.3% on AIME'24) outperforms a Qwen3-8B model trained via distillation from a much larger Qwen3-72B teacher (72.5%).

5.  SRPO is more compute-efficient than GRPO, achieving its reported performance using approximately 3.8 times fewer total training FLOPs.

6.  SRPO mitigates catastrophic forgetting in continual learning scenarios. When an SRPO-trained model is adapted from a math task to a code task, it retains 95.2% of its original math performance, a significantly higher retention rate than models trained with GRPO (87.2%).

7.  The SRPO training process increases the diversity of correct reasoning paths explored by the model. Compared to distillation from a larger external teacher, SRPO leads to a higher number of unique solution paths (4.1 vs. 2.3 per problem) for AIME'24.

8.  The "reset-with-memory" mechanism, where a reflection is prepended to a clean initial state, is critical to SRPO's success. An iterative approach without this state reset results in a 7.0 percentage point performance drop on AIME'24.

9.  The use of reverse KL divergence as the distillation objective is essential for SRPO's effectiveness. Replacing it with forward KL divergence causes a 3.9 percentage point performance drop on AIME'24.

10. Reflections generated by the model itself are more effective training signals within the SRPO framework than reflections generated by a larger, external teacher model, as using an external teacher for reflection degrades performance by 1.8 percentage points on AIME'24.

11. Models trained with SRPO are better able to utilize inference-time self-refinement. Each refinement iteration provides a larger performance boost (+1.8% on AIME'24) for an SRPO-trained model compared to a GRPO-trained model (+1.2%).
