1. The proposed language model system, finetuned on hindsight-guided reasoning (GPT-4o-FT), achieves a superior class-balanced Brier score of 0.151 in forecasting merger outcomes, outperforming calibrated market-implied probabilities (0.199), an XGBoost model (0.186), and the unfinedtuned GPT-4o model with identical context (0.201).

2. The forecasts generated by the finetuned system are less correlated with market-implied probabilities (Pearson $\rho=0.358$) than those from the XGBoost baseline ($\rho=0.753$), suggesting the model provides information not fully captured by market consensus.

3. Finetuning is more effective than post-hoc calibration for improving the model's performance; it reduces the class-balanced calibration error from 0.089 to 0.039, while post-hoc isotonic regression applied to the base model only improves its Brier score to 0.186, still significantly worse than the finetuned model's 0.151.

4. Hindsight-guided supervision is a critical component for the system's performance; removing it during finetuning worsens the class-balanced Brier score from 0.151 to 0.183.

5. The comprehensive context gathered by multiple specialized research agents is essential for accurate forecasting; limiting the model's context to only basic structured "Deal Card" information worsens the class-balanced Brier score from 0.151 to 0.172.

6. The finetuned system is more accurate at predicting the time to deal completion for successful deals, achieving a Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) of 0.385, an improvement over the 0.463 MAPE of the unfinedtuned base model.

7. Finetuning on hindsight-guided reports improves the qualitative reasoning in the generated deal reports, more than doubling the coverage of key decision-relevant issues (from 21.5% to 50.2%) without increasing the rate of unsupported factual claims.

8. The performance of the finetuned forecasting system scales with the size of the training set, as reducing the training data by 50% degrades the class-balanced Brier score from 0.151 to 0.177, indicating performance has not yet saturated.
