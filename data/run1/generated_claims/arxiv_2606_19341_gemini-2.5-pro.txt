Here are the major, unique, and falsifiable claims from the research paper:

1.  The proposed framework, OmniAgent, is the first native omni-modal agent to formulate video understanding as a Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP), unifying perception, reasoning, and action within a single model through an iterative Observation-Thought-Action (OTA) cycle.

2.  OmniAgent's architecture, which selectively distills high-dimensional audio-visual information into a persistent textual memory while purging the raw media, effectively decouples the model's reasoning complexity and context cost from the raw duration of the video.

3.  Standard Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) suffers from an "Advantage Homogenization" problem in multi-turn agentic reasoning, where a uniform trajectory-level reward signal fails to assign appropriate credit to pivotal discovery turns.

4.  The proposed reinforcement learning algorithm, TAURA (Turn-aware Adaptive Uncertainty Rescaled Advantage), resolves the advantage homogenization problem by using turn-level token entropy to rescale the advantage, which steers credit assignment toward high-uncertainty decision points.

5.  OmniAgent exhibits positive "test-time scaling," meaning its performance on complex video understanding tasks improves as the maximum allowed number of reasoning turns is increased during inference.

6.  Agentic Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), which involves synthesizing best-of-N trajectories and filtering them with a dual-stage quality control (outcome verification and rationality audit), is more effective at bootstrapping active perception capabilities than standard SFT on static question-answer pairs.

7.  A 7B parameter OmniAgent outperforms a 10x larger, 72B parameter passive model (Qwen2.5-VL-72B) on the long-video LVBench benchmark.

8.  OmniAgent achieves higher accuracy on long-video benchmarks than passive models while being more sample-efficient, using significantly fewer frames for its analysis (e.g., 73% fewer frames than Qwen2.5-VL-72B on LVBench).

9.  The iterative, coarse-to-fine search strategy employed by OmniAgent results in substantial performance gains on temporal grounding tasks (e.g., a +33.4% absolute gain on LongVALE over its baseline), outperforming methods that regress coordinates from a single global view.
