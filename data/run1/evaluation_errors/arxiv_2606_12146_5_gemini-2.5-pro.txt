The most serious technical flaw is a contradiction in the core mathematical derivation of nD-RoPE. The derivation begins by imposing the condition that at zero relative displacement, the inner product of position-aware embeddings must reduce to the standard content-based inner product, `qᵀk` (first excerpt). For this to equal `qᵀk`, the matrix `W` must be orthogonal (`WᵀW = I`). This critical constraint is never stated or enforced in the paper, creating a logical gap between the theoretical foundation and the practical implementation.
