Error in assumptions (core conceptual)
error:
Once triggered, we update the world model by grounding its foresight in the current observation. This is achieved by minimizing a reconstruction loss that encourages the predicted future state $\tilde{\boldsymbol{s}}_{t+1}$ to reconstruct the current calibrated visual features $\boldsymbol{\hat{v}}_t$, thereby correcting for predictive drift:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:grad}
\boldsymbol{\Theta} \leftarrow \boldsymbol{\Theta} - \eta \cdot \,\nabla_{\small \boldsymbol{\Theta}} \|\, d(\tilde{\boldsymbol{s}}_{t+1}) - \boldsymbol{\hat{v}}_t \|_2^2,
\end{equation}
where $\eta$ denotes the adaptation learning rate and we denote all components of the world model without policies collectively by parameters $\boldsymbol{\Theta}$.
