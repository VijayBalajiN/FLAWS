As described in Section~\ref{sec:pooled}, value collapse is the progressive displacement of HTL-intensive production when undifferentiated evaluation fails to reward its higher costs. A related failure mode is \emph{model collapse}, where repeated training on model-generated data degrades distributional coverage \citep{shumailov2024curserecursiontraininggenerated, alemohammad2024selfconsuming, schaeffer2025positionmodelcollapsedoes}. Value collapse is a primary driver of model collapse by shifting pool composition. As value collapse forces HTL-intensive producers to adopt AI-assisted workflows, their outputs—while still human-guided—become part of the growing pool of synthetic data. This AI-assisted content, lacking the implicit, unarticulated knowledge embedded in pure HTL work, is the primary source of data contamination that drives model collapse. By training on this homogenized, AI-mediated data, next-generation models fail to learn the full richness of the true data distribution.
