The original plan supports this by describing self-attention mechanisms where each position can attend to all other positions in the sequence, which is the mechanism for capturing these global dependencies.

The modification introduces a self-contradiction by altering the definition of the core self-attention mechanism. It states that each position is "restricted to attend only to positions within a fixed-size local window". This change directly cripples the very mechanism intended to capture global dependencies, making it inherently local. By its own modified definition, the architecture can now only model local dependencies, directly violating the hypothesis's stated goal of modeling global dependencies. The proposed machinery breaks its own high-level objective.
