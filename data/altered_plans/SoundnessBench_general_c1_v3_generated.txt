:original_text:
exhibiting a bias towards optimism and high sensitivity to prompt framing

:modified_text:
exhibiting a bias towards optimism in its textual ratings and high sensitivity to prompt framing

:explanation:
This modification introduces an error of construct invalidity. The claim is that LLMs are optimistic about the *soundness* of proposals. This modified text subtly shifts the object of the optimism to the "textual ratings" themselves. This implies the model is not making a flawed judgment about the scientific merit, but rather that its rating scale or mechanism is inherently skewed positive. The model might be accurately rating a different construct, such as rhetorical quality or structural coherence, which the researchers have then misinterpreted as a biased evaluation of soundness.
