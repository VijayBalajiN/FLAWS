The mechanism of error is a failure to use the correct network depth for the representation.

The embedding for a regular word is not trained for this purpose and is a much weaker choice for a sentence representation. This flawed design would test a different, less effective hypothesis, and its likely poor results would be misinterpreted as evidence against the standard, correct `[CLS]` token methodology. The mechanism of error is a misunderstanding of the architectural role of special tokens.
