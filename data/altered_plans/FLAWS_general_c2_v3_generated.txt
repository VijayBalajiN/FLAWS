:modified_text:
This will mitigate model-specific biases in error generation by using a large language model and its corresponding instruct-tuned version.

:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is the introduction of a **confounding variable**. The claim is that the method mitigates *model-specific* bias, but the modified text reveals that the comparison is between a base model and its instruct-tuned version. The observed differences in generated errors are now confounded; it is impossible to disentangle the effect of the base model's characteristics from the effect of the instruct-tuning process. The benchmark would be measuring a combination of two different variables, making the claim to have isolated and mitigated "model-specific" biases false.
