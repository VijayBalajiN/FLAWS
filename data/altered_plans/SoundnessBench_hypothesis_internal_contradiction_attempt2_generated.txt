:error_type:
Internal Contradiction

:original_text:
Large Language Models' assessment of a research proposal's methodological soundness is unreliable, exhibiting a bias towards optimism and high sensitivity to prompt framing.

:modified_text:
Large Language Models' assessment of a research proposal's methodological soundness is unreliable, exhibiting a stable bias towards optimism and high sensitivity to prompt framing.

:explanation:
The modification introduces a contradiction between two of the hypothesis's core claims: a "stable bias" and "high sensitivity."

1.  The hypothesis claims the LLM's assessment exhibits a **stable** bias towards optimism. A stable property or bias is, by definition, one that is robust, persistent, and resistant to minor perturbations in its conditions.
2.  The hypothesis simultaneously claims the assessment exhibits **high sensitivity** to prompt framing. This means that minor variations in the input prompt can cause significant changes in the model's judgment.

The contradiction is that a bias cannot be both "stable" and "highly sensitive" to framing. If the bias can be easily altered or even reversed by changing the prompt (the mechanism of sensitivity), then it is inherently unstable. The hypothesis's own stated mechanism (high sensitivity) directly refutes its own stated property (stability), creating a self-contained logical flaw.
