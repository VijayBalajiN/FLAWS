:original_text:
We will impose constraints on the maximum number and length of excerpts to prevent trivial solutions.

:modified_text:
We will impose constraints on the maximum number and sentence length of excerpts to prevent trivial solutions.

:explanation:
This version introduces an error by using an inappropriate and easily gameable unit of measurement. "Sentence length" is not a robust metric for constraining text quantity. A model could find a single, very long, or run-on sentence that contains all the necessary information, effectively bypassing the spirit of the constraint. Because the chosen unit is too coarse and variable, the constraint is not an effective method for preventing simplistic, copy-paste solutions, thus falsifying the claim.
