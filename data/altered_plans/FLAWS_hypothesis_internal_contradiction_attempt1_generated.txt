:error_type:
Internal Contradiction

:original_text:
For each successfully inserted error, we will prompt an LLM to identify all text excerpts in the paper that are rendered incorrect by the modification. This complete set of excerpts will serve as the ground truth for our evaluation.

:modified_text:
For each successfully inserted error, we will prompt the same error-generating LLM to identify all text excerpts in the paper that are rendered incorrect by the modification. This complete set of excerpts will serve as the ground truth for our evaluation.

:explanation:
The contradiction arises from the interaction between two steps in the proposed methodology: the error filtering process and the ground truth generation process.

1.  **The Filtering Mechanism:** The methodology specifies a filter called "internal identification" where the "error-generating LLM" is tasked with finding the error it just created. If the LLM succeeds, the error is discarded as too simple. Therefore, every single error that makes it into the final benchmark is, by definition, an error that the error-generating LLM was unable to find.

2.  **The Ground Truth Mechanism (Modified):** The modified text specifies that this *same* error-generating LLM is then used to create the ground truth by identifying all text excerpts that constitute the error.

3.  **The Internal Contradiction:** The methodology now requires the same LLM to be simultaneously incapable of finding the error (to pass the filter) and perfectly capable of identifying the complete and precise location of that same error (to generate the ground truth). The precondition for an error's inclusion in the benchmark (the LLM's failure to find it) makes the subsequent step of creating a reliable ground truth with that same LLM logically impossible. The mechanism for ensuring error difficulty directly invalidates the mechanism for ensuring ground truth reliability.
