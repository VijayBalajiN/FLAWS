:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is **data leakage from an alternative source**. This modification narrows the check to a bureaucratic event (assignment of an arXiv ID) rather than the first public appearance of the content. A paper's text could have existed on a researcher's personal website, a workshop page, or a different preprint server for months before being formally submitted to arXiv. Web-crawling for LLM training data would likely have captured these earlier, informal versions, thus contaminating the test set even though the arXiv ID is "new".

:modified_text:
ensuring their most recent revision on arXiv was posted after the knowledge cutoff dates of the models we plan to test.

:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is **temporal data leakage via versioning**. The flaw is substituting the date of the "most recent revision" for the "first posted" date. A paper could have its first version (v1) posted before the knowledge cutoff, with its core ideas and text becoming part of the training data. A later revision (v2, v3) with minor changes or additions posted after the cutoff would be incorrectly selected as "new" data, leading to contamination. A researcher might plausibly make this mistake while trying to use the most "up-to-date" version of a paper for evaluation.

:modified_text:
ensuring the paper’s novel results were first posted on arXiv after the knowledge cutoff dates of the models we plan to test.

:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is **indirect contamination through content overlap**. This modification shifts the evaluation criterion from the novelty of the entire document to the novelty of only its "results". A paper could be published after the cutoff date with new results, but its entire methodology, background, and literature review sections could be copied nearly verbatim from a prior work by the same authors that was published before the cutoff. The LLM would have been trained on the methodological text, allowing it to "cheat" by recognizing the experimental setup, even if the final results are new to it.
