high sensitivity to prompt framing

:modified_text:
high compliance with prompt framing

:explanation:
This error mischaracterizes the effect. The claim states the LLM's judgment is "unstable," implying unpredictable or chaotic variance. "Compliance" suggests a predictable, stable bias instead. For example, a prompt framed to be "critical" will consistently produce a critical review, while one framed to be "supportive" will consistently produce a supportive one. This is a predictable, systematic bias, not instability. By replacing "sensitivity" with "compliance," the flaw redefines the observed phenomenon in a way that directly contradicts the claim of "instability."
