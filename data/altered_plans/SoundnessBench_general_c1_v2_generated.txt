This modification introduces an error of overgeneralization. The original claim is about "Large Language Models" as a broad category. By specifying that the bias is observed "in instruction-tuned models," the text now suggests the finding may not be generalizable to all LLMs (e.g., base models). The claim is thus falsified because the phenomenon is not a property of LLMs in general, but a characteristic of a specific, non-representative subset, likely due to their fine-tuning for helpful and agreeable dialogue.
