We will frame this as a classification problem (binary: Accept/Reject; ternary: Reject/Poster/Highlight) and measure performance using the Accuracy score.

:explanation:
The mechanism of error is the selection of a metric that is inappropriate for the likely data distribution. By removing "macro F1 score," the evaluation relies solely on Accuracy. The specified classes (e.g., Reject/Poster/Highlight) are almost certain to be severely imbalanced in a real-world setting, with "Reject" being the vast majority class. Accuracy is a notoriously misleading metric for imbalanced datasets, as a useless model that only predicts the majority class can achieve a very high score. This makes the measurement not meaningful.
