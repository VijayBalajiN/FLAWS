:original_text:
The criticality of an error in a scientific paper is a function of its connection to the paper's core claims.

:modified_text:
The criticality of an error in a scientific paper, defined by its proximity to the paper's core claims, is a function of its connection to those same claims.

:explanation:
This flaw is based on circular reasoning (begging the question). By defining "criticality" within the premise using the exact same concept as the conclusion ("proximity/connection to the paper's core claims"), the statement becomes a tautology. It essentially argues that "the connection to the claims is a function of the connection to the claims," which is logically empty and unfalsifiable, thereby failing to make the meaningful empirical assertion of the original claim.
