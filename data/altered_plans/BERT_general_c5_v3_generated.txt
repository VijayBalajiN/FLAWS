:explanation:
The flaw's mechanism is a confounding variable introduced by an architectural mismatch. The standard fine-tuning approach for BERT on NER adds only a simple linear layer on top of the transformer. By pairing the feature-based approach with a powerful, specialized BiLSTM-CRF sequence tagging model, the experiment is no longer a fair comparison of transfer learning strategies. It now compares "frozen BERT + powerful classifier" against "fine-tuned BERT + simple classifier." Any observed performance difference could be due to the addition of the BiLSTM-CRF, not the superiority of the feature-based method itself, thus invalidating the experiment's conclusion about the claim.
