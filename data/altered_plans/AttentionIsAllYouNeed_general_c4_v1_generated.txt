:modified_text:
We will employ label smoothing with a value of ϵ_ls = 0.1 during training, in conjunction with our chosen focal loss function, as this approach is known to improve model robustness.

:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is an incompatible combination of techniques. Focal loss is designed to focus training on hard-to-classify examples by down-weighting the loss assigned to well-classified examples. Label smoothing, conversely, regularizes the model by penalizing overconfident predictions on all examples. Applying them together is non-standard and their effects can be contradictory; label smoothing may soften the very probabilities that focal loss uses to identify "well-classified" examples, thereby interfering with the focal loss mechanism.
