:explanation:
The mechanism of error is methodological incoherence, where a scientific-sounding term is applied in a nonsensical context. "Rank-normalized Accuracy" is a meaningless phrase. Accuracy is a single scalar value computed over a dataset (or fold); one cannot "rank-normalize" a single number. This error suggests a superficial "cargo cult" understanding of statistics, where a procedure is named without understanding its meaning or applicability. This renders the proposed measurement framework ill-defined and therefore not meaningful.
