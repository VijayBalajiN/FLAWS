:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is misattribution of cause. The modified text incorrectly attributes the representations' power to the presence of "multi-head self-attention mechanisms" instead of the original's "jointly conditioned on both left and right context." This is a subtle but critical flaw because many powerful unidirectional models (e.g., GPT-series) also use multi-head self-attention in all layers. The comparison is therefore invalidated, as the feature claimed to provide the advantage is also present in the group it is being compared against, making the hypothesis logically unsound.
