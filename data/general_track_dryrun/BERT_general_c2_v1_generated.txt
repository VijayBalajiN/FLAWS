:modified_text:
This will allow us to isolate the contributions of deep bidirectionality and the NSP task, which necessitates a smaller learning rate.

:explanation:
The mechanism of this error is the introduction of a confounding variable. By stating that the removal of the NSP task "necessitates" a change in another key hyperparameter (the learning rate), the experimental design is compromised. Any observed difference in performance can no longer be attributed solely to the NSP task; it could be due to the NSP task, the different learning rate, or an interaction between the two. This makes it impossible to "isolate the contributions" of the NSP task as claimed.
